Rayleigh Scattering (Pre-Veil)

In clear air, microscopic nitrogen and oxygen molecules scatter shorter blue wavelengths (\(\sim 450\text{ nm}\)) ten times more than red light. This creates the breathtaking, luminous cerulean sky that human eyes evolved beneath for three million years.

Mie Aerosol Scattering (Post-Veil)

Sub-micron sulfate aerosols (\(\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4\)) scatter all wavelengths uniformly. The crisp blue dissolves into an opaque, milky-zinc glare. Shadows become fuzzy smudges, and the emotional sensation of direct radiant heat on skin is completely extinguished.
